Boy to Ballon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Boy to Ballon ("the app") is published by Insane Utils. Contact: insaneutils@gmail.com

What the app stores. Your careers, Hall of Fame and settings are saved on your device and, if you are signed in to Play Games, backed up to your Google account (below). We have no accounts and collect no names, emails or contacts. Apart from ads and Google Play Games (below), the app sends nothing over the internet.

Ads. The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a small banner on two screens, an occasional full-screen ad between seasons or careers, and rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. In the EEA/UK you are asked for consent first, and ads are non-personalised without it; you can change your choice any time under Settings > Ad privacy options. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google Play Games. If you are signed in to Play Games, your daily-challenge score is posted to the game's public leaderboard under your Play Games name. Achievements you earn are unlocked on your Play Games profile, and a copy of your careers, Hall of Fame and achievements is kept in Play Games saved games so a new phone can restore them. Only you can read that save. This is handled by Google under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy The game also shows today's top 10 scores from that leaderboard (Play Games names and scores) inside the app.

Notifications. If you switch on the daily challenge reminder, the app schedules one local notification a day on your device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and you can switch it off in Settings.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes. Updates to this policy will be posted at this address.
